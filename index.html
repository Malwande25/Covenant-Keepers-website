In Action

Key Initiatives

✦
  • ⚽
    Presidential Cup Soccer Tournament
    Our flagship annual event bringing together regions in healthy, God-honouring competition that builds brotherhood across boundaries.
  • 🏫
    Reviving School Sports in Townships & Rural Areas
    Restoring organised sports to communities where youth desperately need healthy outlets, mentorship, and structure.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    National Family Day (gala Dinner) — Every Year
    A celebration of fathers as the anchors of their families, promoting unity, responsibility, and renewed purpose.
  • 🛡️
    Substance Abuse Recovery Support
    Walking alongside men battling addiction — offering community, accountability, and the hope of full restoration.
