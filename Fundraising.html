Why Your Support Matters

✦

Covenant Keepers operates entirely through the generosity of individuals, churches, and organisations who believe in the power of transformed men. Your gift directly funds programmes that reach fathers, youth, and communities that desperately need hope and direction.

Other Ways to Help

Beyond Financial Giving

✦
  • 🎽
    Donate Sports Equipment
    Gently used soccer boots, jerseys, balls, and training equipment are always welcome. Contact us to arrange a drop-off.
  • 🏢
    Corporate Partnership
    Partner your business with Covenant Keepers for sponsorship, CSI alignment, or employee volunteer days.
  • 🎤
    Host a Fundraising Event
    Organise a dinner, fun run, or community event and pledge the proceeds to one of our campaigns.
  • 🙏
    Pray & Advocate
    Share our work with your church, network, or community. Awareness is a gift that costs nothing and opens many doors.
