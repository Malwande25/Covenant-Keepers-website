Skills Development

Equipping men to provide, lead, and leave a legacy

Dignity Through Work

A Man Who Can Provide

✦

Unemployment and economic marginalisation rob men of dignity and purpose. When a man cannot provide for his family, it erodes his sense of identity and his role in the home. Covenant Keepers addresses this at the root.

Our Skills Development programmes equip men with practical vocational training, entrepreneurial foundations, and financial literacy — the building blocks of self-sufficiency and dignified provision.

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters."
Colossians 3:23
Vocational Training

What We Teach

✦
🔨

Construction & Building Trades

Bricklaying, plastering, plumbing basics, and electrical safety — skills in high demand across communities and construction sites.

🚗

Motor Mechanics

Basic vehicle maintenance and diagnostics — enabling men to work independently or gain employment in the transport sector.

💻

Digital Literacy

Computer skills, Camera installation, internet literacy, and basic digital tools that open doors to formal employment and online business opportunities.

🌱

Agricultural Skills

Food gardening, small-scale farming, and permaculture principles that support household food security and small enterprise.

💼

Entrepreneurship Basics

Business planning, pricing, customer management, and legal registration — empowering men to start and sustain small businesses.

💰

Financial Literacy

Budgeting, debt management, saving habits, and understanding financial products — tools to build lasting household stability.
