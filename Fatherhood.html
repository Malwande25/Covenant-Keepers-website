Fatherhood

Raising fathers who lead with faith, love, and courage

The Heart of Our Mission

Fathers: The Anchor of the Home

✦

The absence of engaged, present fathers is one of the defining crises of our generation. Covenant Keepers exists to reverse this — to call men back to the sacred responsibility of fatherhood.

We do not only address physical absence. We equip men to be emotionally present, spiritually engaged, and actively involved in the shaping of their children's lives and futures.

"Fathers, do not exasperate your children; instead, bring them up in the training and instruction of the Lord."
Ephesians 6:4
Flagship Event

Regional Family Day — Every September

✦

Every September, Covenant Keepers hosts Regional Family Days that celebrate fathers as the anchors of their families — a day of worship, affirmation, fun, and renewed commitment.

🎉

A full day of games, sports, and shared experiences that rebuild family bonds and create lasting memories.

🎤

🙏

Corporate Worship

Equipping Fathers

Fatherhood Curriculum

✦
  • 📖
    Biblical Foundations of Fatherhood
    Exploring what Scripture says about the father's role — as provider, protector, priest, and prophet in the home.
  • 💬
    Communication & Emotional Availability
    Practical tools for building open, trusting relationships with children and spouses.
  • ⚖️
    Discipline with Dignity
    Learning to correct, correct lovingly, set boundaries, and model consistency without harshness.
  • 💰
    Financial Stewardship for Families
    Budgeting, providing, and setting a financial example that your children will carry for life.
  • 🌍
    Legacy & Generational Impact
    Understanding that fatherhood is not a season — it is a legacy. What are you building for the generations to come?
Transformed Lives

What Restoration Looks Like

✦

Through our Fatherhood programmes, many men have reclaimed their roles as protectors of their households. Children who previously turned to the streets now have fathers who show up — at dinner tables, school gates, and bedside prayers.

This is not a statistic. It is the slow, faithful work of restoration — one man, one family, one community at a time.

"He will turn the hearts of the parents to their children, and the hearts of the children to their parents."
Malachi 4:6
Covenant Keepers

Restoring moral fibre through Christ-centred initiatives. Developing men of faith, character, and community.
