Community Footprint

Transforming communities from within — one man at a time

Rooted in Service

Leaving a Mark That Matters

✦

A man transformed by faith does not keep that transformation to himself. He takes it home, to his street, to his school, and to his region. Covenant Keepers exists to channel that energy into organised, sustained community impact.

From rural schools to urban townships, our community programmes bring structure, hope, and the love of Christ into places where it is needed most.

Flagship Programme

The Presidential Cup Soccer Tournament

✦

Our annual Presidential Cup brings together regions across the country in healthy, God-honouring competition. More than a tournament, it is a gathering of brothers — men who compete on the field and build each other up off it.

The Cup has grown into one of the most anticipated events in our calendar, drawing participants, families, and supporters from across the nation.

Annual Event Multiple Regions All Ages Welcome
Youth Empowerment

Reviving School Sports

✦

Many schools in townships and rural areas have lost their sports programmes due to lack of funding, facilities, and volunteer capacity. Covenant Keepers is changing that — one school at a time.

🏟️

Field Restoration

Partnering with local communities to restore and maintain sports fields that give youth a safe, organised space to play and grow.

👨‍🏫

Volunteer Coaches

Recruiting and training men to serve as volunteer coaches — providing youth with mentors who model discipline, respect, and faith.

🎽

Equipment Drives

Collecting and distributing sporting equipment to schools that cannot afford basic kit — ensuring no child is left on the sideline.

📚

Sport & Study Balance

Encouraging children to focus on both education and sport rather than the streets — sport as a pathway to discipline and academic engagement.
