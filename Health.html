Health & Wellness

Caring for the temple God entrusted to you

Whole-Man Wellbeing

Body, Mind & Spirit

✦

Covenant Keepers believes that a man's health is foundational to his purpose. We cannot lead our families, serve our communities, or honour God when we neglect the bodies and minds He has given us.

Our Health & Wellness portfolio equips men with practical tools and spiritual grounding to pursue wholeness in every area of life — physical fitness, mental resilience, and spiritual vitality.

"Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore honour God with your bodies."
1 Corinthians 6:19–20
What We Offer

Health Programmes

✦
⚽

Sport & Physical Fitness

Regular sporting activities including soccer, running events, and team sports designed to foster discipline, brotherhood, and physical wellbeing.

🧠

Mental Health Awareness

Breaking the stigma around men's mental health through open conversations, trained facilitators, and safe spaces for vulnerability and healing.

🛡️

Substance Abuse Recovery

A structured support network for men battling addiction — offering accountability partners, counselling referrals, and a community of restored men.

🍎

Nutrition & Lifestyle

Practical workshops on healthy eating, sleep, and lifestyle habits — helping men make sustainable choices for long-term vitality.

🙏

Spiritual Wellness

Bible study groups, prayer walks, and devotional disciplines that nourish the inner man and anchor daily life in God's Word.

🏥

Men's Health Screenings

Partnering with healthcare providers to offer free or subsidised health checks — because real strength includes knowing your numbers.

A Particular Focus

Walking Men Through Recovery

✦

Substance abuse has torn apart countless families across our nation. Covenant Keepers refuses to look away. Our recovery support initiatives provide men a structured, faith-based pathway out of addiction.

Many men in our community have reclaimed their roles as protectors of their households through these programmes. Their restoration is a testimony to what is possible when men are surrounded by truth and brotherhood.
