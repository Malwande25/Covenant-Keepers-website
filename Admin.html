The Office of Administration

Governing with Integrity

✦

The Secretary General Office serves as the administrative heart of Covenant Keepers — coordinating regional operations, maintaining governance standards, managing membership records, and ensuring that our programmes run with excellence and accountability.

We are committed to transparent, faith-based stewardship of every resource entrusted to us — whether time, finances, or people.
